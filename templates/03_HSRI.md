# #2 Health Systems Research Institute (HSRI / สวรส.) – Application Template

**Why it fits:** HSRI funds research that improves the Thai health system. An AI screening tool used in district hospitals or by primary-care staff, with cost-effectiveness data, is exactly its kind of project.
**Website:** https://www.hsri.or.th (research framework: hsri.or.th/research_grant/research_framework)
**Submission:** NRIIS plus your institution's research-management system. Thai language.
**Typical cycle:** the annual call has opened around **mid-Feb to end of Apr** (the FY2569 call ran 17 Feb–30 Apr 2025). The **FY2571** call is expected around Feb–Apr 2027 **[CONFIRM]**. HSRI also sometimes runs targeted calls during the year.

---

## A. Eligibility check
- ☐ PI at a Thai university, hospital, or government health agency **[CONFIRM]**
- ☐ Registered in NRIIS
- ☐ Project fits one of HSRI's **programmes (แผนงาน)** for that year. Recent ones include health service system development, health equity, and health emergency response. Choose the programme your project fits best and use its wording
- ☐ Choose the **correct proposal form**. HSRI provides separate forms, e.g. **health systems research (งานวิจัยเชิงระบบ)** vs **clinical research (งานวิจัยทางคลินิก)**. For an AI screening + implementation project, use the clinical form if the core is diagnostic accuracy, or the systems form if the core is the service model and scale-up

## D. Fill-in template (typical HSRI proposal headings)

**1. ชื่อโครงการ (Title, TH/EN):** ____

**2. ความสำคัญและที่มาของปัญหา (Problem & health-system significance).** Frame it as a **health-system** problem, not only a clinical one:
- Late diagnosis burden and its cost to UCS/NHSO: ____
- Gaps in screening coverage at primary-care level: ____
- Workforce shortage of oral specialists in your provinces: ____

**3. คำถามการวิจัย (Research questions)**
1. How accurate is the AI tool compared with specialists, in Thai settings?
2. Can non-specialists (dentists, nurses, village health volunteers) use it within the existing referral system?
3. What is the cost per early cancer detected, and is it cost-effective for the Thai UCS?

**4. วัตถุประสงค์ (Objectives):** CP §5, re-worded toward the health system

**5. กรอบแนวคิด (Conceptual framework):** diagram from community screening → AI triage → referral → biopsy → treatment, with your indicators at each step

**6. ระเบียบวิธีวิจัย (Methods):** CP §6, **plus**
- Health-economic evaluation (cost-effectiveness / budget impact, from the payer's perspective)
- Implementation outcomes: reach, adoption, fidelity, acceptability
- Policy analysis: how the tool could enter the MOPH screening package

**7. ผลที่คาดว่าจะได้รับและการนำไปใช้ (Expected results & policy use):** who in MOPH, NHSO or the provinces will use the findings, and how

**8. แผนการดำเนินงาน (Work plan):** CP §8

**9. งบประมาณ (Budget):** CP §9, in HSRI categories

**10. การเผยแพร่และผลักดันเชิงนโยบาย (Dissemination & policy translation):** policy brief, stakeholder meeting, publications

## E. Budget template
Same Thai government categories as NRCT (compensation, wages, materials, operating, equipment, overhead). HSRI is generally reluctant to fund **major equipment**, so justify it or source it elsewhere (e.g. compute credits, file 06). **[CONFIRM]**

## F. Scoring tips
- Lead with **health-system impact and policy use**, not AI novelty.
- Include **cost-effectiveness** and **equity** (rural, low-income, high-risk groups).
- Get a letter from someone who could **adopt** the tool (provincial health office, NHSO region, MOPH).
- Use the exact wording of HSRI's programme in your objectives.
