# #7 UICC (Union for International Cancer Control) Fellowships & Grants – Application Template

**Why it fits:** small, achievable awards for cancer professionals in countries like Thailand. They pay for **training visits** (e.g. a team member spending weeks at an institution with oral-cancer AI or screening expertise) and capacity building. They build the track record that larger grants want to see.
**Website:** https://www.uicc.org/what-we-do/capacity-building/grants
**Submission:** UICC online application portal **[CONFIRM]**
**Cycle:** fellowship rounds open several times a year or annually, depending on the programme **[CONFIRM the programmes running in 2026–27]**

---

## A. Eligibility check
- ☐ Applicant is a cancer professional (clinician, researcher, nurse, public-health or cancer-control professional) **[CONFIRM career-stage limits]**
- ☐ Applicant's organisation may need to be a **UICC member**, or membership may be an advantage. Check whether any of your 4 hospitals (or NCI Thailand) is a member **[CONFIRM]**
- ☐ A **host institution abroad** has agreed to receive you (for exchange-type fellowships)
- ☐ The project produces a concrete benefit for your home institution on return

## C. Fill-in template – project / learning plan

**1. Title:** e.g. "Learning AI-assisted oral cancer screening methods to implement in a 4-hospital Thai network"

**2. Background (≈ ½ page):** oral cancer burden in Thailand (CP §4); your hospital's current screening practice; the gap

**3. Objectives of the fellowship (3–4)**
1. Learn ____ (e.g. image-acquisition standards, annotation, AI validation methods, screening programme design) at ____
2. Establish a collaboration for ____ (joint dataset, publication, future grant)
3. ____

**4. Host institution & why:** expertise, ongoing programmes, named supervisor

**5. Activities & schedule:** week-by-week plan

**6. Expected outcomes & application at home**
- Protocol(s) adopted at your 4 hospitals: ____
- Staff you will train on return: ____
- Joint outputs: ____

**7. Sustainability:** how this links to the larger project and grants (NRCT, HSRI, NIH)

## E. Tips
- A **specific host and supervisor** with a strong acceptance letter matters most.
- Show a **clear plan to apply the learning** at your hospitals.
- Keep the budget realistic and within the cap.
