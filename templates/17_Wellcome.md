# #16 Wellcome Trust – Application Template

**Why it fits:** large, flexible funding, and many schemes accept researchers based in Thailand. Very competitive; best once you have pilot results.
**Website:** https://wellcome.org/grant-funding · **Submission:** Wellcome Funding Platform (online)
**Relevant schemes [CONFIRM current list, eligibility and deadlines]:**
| Scheme | For | Typical scale |
|---|---|---|
| **Early-Career Awards** | Researchers early in their independent career | Salary + research costs, ~5 yrs |
| **Career Development Awards** | Mid-career researchers building an independent programme | Larger, ~8 yrs |
| **Discovery Awards** | Established researchers with a bold research programme | Large, ~8 yrs |
| Thematic calls (e.g. data science / digital health, if open) | Specific topics | Varies |

**Important:** Wellcome Discovery Research funds **discovery**: new knowledge, bold questions. Pure implementation or product development is a weaker fit. Frame it around a **research question**, e.g. "What visual and biological features predict malignant transformation of oral lesions, and can AI capture them in low-resource settings?"

---

## A. Eligibility check
- ☐ Host organisation in an eligible country and eligible to hold Wellcome grants (Thai universities usually qualify; check whether your hospital does, or apply via an affiliated university) **[CONFIRM]**
- ☐ Applicant career stage fits the scheme
- ☐ Applicant has (or will have) a contract with the host for the award's duration
- ☐ The research is in Wellcome's remit (human health; discovery research)

## C. Fill-in: research proposal skeleton
1. **Vision (½ page):** the big question and why it matters for LMICs
2. **Aims (3):** ____ / ____ / ____
3. **Approach:** design, data (4-hospital network), AI methods, validation, risks
4. **Preliminary work:** CP §7. Wellcome expects evidence you can deliver
5. **Timeline (5–8 yrs):** phased plan
6. **Impact & future direction:** where the programme leads

## D. Tips
- Wellcome emphasises the **person and the idea**: write a compelling **career narrative**.
- **Contact Wellcome early** (some schemes have eligibility enquiries).
- Show **positive research culture**: training, inclusion, collaboration.

## E. Verify [CONFIRM]
- ☐ Scheme deadlines (Wellcome runs several rounds a year)
- ☐ Host eligibility and career-stage criteria
