# #19 Gates Foundation Grand Challenges / Google.org – Application Template

Both offer large but **occasional, highly competitive** calls. Keep a **ready-to-adapt 2-page concept** so you can respond quickly when a relevant call opens.

---

## Part A – Gates Foundation Grand Challenges
**Website:** https://gcgh.grandchallenges.org · **Cycle:** topic-specific calls, a few per year **[CONFIRM open calls; watch for "AI for global health" or LMIC diagnostics topics]**
**Note:** Gates priorities centre on infectious disease, maternal & child health, and nutrition. **Cancer is usually outside scope** unless a call explicitly includes NCDs or general AI-for-health. Apply **only** when the call topic fits.

### Eligibility check
- ☐ Call topic explicitly covers your work (AI tools for LMIC health workers, diagnostics, etc.)
- ☐ Organisation type eligible (universities, hospitals and NGOs usually are)

### Typical submission (short online proposal) [CONFIRM per call]
| Component | Limit | Done |
|---|---|---|
| Short proposal answering the call's questions | ~2 pages / set character limits | ☐ |
| Applicant & organisation details | form | ☐ |
| Budget estimate (often a fixed amount, e.g. Phase I seed grant) | form | ☐ |
| (Usually **no preliminary data required**; boldness and novelty are valued) | | |

### Fill-in: 2-page concept
1. **The problem (≈ 150 words):** ____
2. **The idea – what's new (≈ 250 words):** ____
3. **How you will test it (≈ 250 words):** plan, success criteria, timeline (often 12–24 months)
4. **Why it could transform health in LMICs (≈ 150 words):** scale, cost, equity
5. **Team (≈ 100 words):** ____

## Part B – Google.org
**Website:** https://www.google.org · **Cycle:** periodic "Impact Challenge" and "AI for Social Good / Accelerator" calls; some by invitation **[CONFIRM current open calls]**

### Typical requirements [CONFIRM]
| Component | Done |
|---|---|
| Organisation eligibility (often **non-profits / charitable organisations**, social enterprises, academic institutions; registered legal entity) | ☐ |
| Application form: problem, AI solution, impact metrics, scalability, team, budget | ☐ |
| Evidence of traction (pilot users, data access, partners) | ☐ |
| Responsible-AI considerations (bias, privacy, safety) | ☐ |
| Open-source / sharing commitments | ☐ |

### Fill-in
- **Problem & who's affected:** ____ people at risk in ____ provinces
- **AI solution:** ____ (what model, what users, offline/low-bandwidth?)
- **Impact metrics:** people screened, early cases found, cost per detection
- **Path to scale:** MOPH adoption, other ASEAN countries
- **Responsible AI:** subgroup performance, privacy, human-in-the-loop referral
- **Budget & use of funds:** ____

## Tips
- Keep a **1-page fact sheet + 2-page concept + short video demo** ready.
- Sign up for both funders' newsletters to catch calls early.
