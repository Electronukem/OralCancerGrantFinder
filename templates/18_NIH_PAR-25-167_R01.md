## Fit
✅ In scope: trials of **screening and early detection**, including the "operating characteristics and clinical impact" of early-detection technologies; health-care delivery; implementation science.
❌ Not in scope: **Phase III imaging or other diagnostic trials**; Phase 0–II medical imaging trials (→ PAR-25-081, template 19); diagnosis studies in patients; observational studies.

**Best framing (later stage, after pilot):** a **pragmatic or cluster-randomised trial** of AI-assisted community screening vs usual care across the 4 hospitals' catchment areas, measuring **stage at diagnosis, time to diagnosis, referral completion, cost-effectiveness**.
