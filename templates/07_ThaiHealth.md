# #6 Thai Health Promotion Foundation (ThaiHealth / สสส.) – Application Template

**Why it fits:** ThaiHealth funds **health promotion and prevention**, not lab or AI development. Fund the **community screening and risk-reduction** part of the project here: training village health volunteers (อสม.), awareness campaigns about tobacco, alcohol and betel quid, and community screening days using the AI app.
**Website:** https://www.thaihealth.or.th
**Submission:** ThaiHealth online grant system (open grant / โครงการทั่วไป, or programme-specific calls). Thai language. **[CONFIRM current channel]**

## C. Fill-in template (typical headings)

**1. ชื่อโครงการ:** e.g. "Community oral cancer screening and risk-reduction with AI-assisted village health volunteers in ____ province"

**2. หลักการและเหตุผล (Rationale):** risk-factor prevalence in the target community (smoking, drinking, betel quid), late diagnosis, low screening access (CP §4)

**3. วัตถุประสงค์ (Objectives)**
1. Train ____ village health volunteers / community staff in oral self-exam and AI-assisted photo screening
2. Screen ____ high-risk adults in ____ sub-districts
3. Refer ____% of positives to a partner hospital within ____ days
4. Reduce risk behaviour (quit smoking/betel referrals) by ____

**4. กลุ่มเป้าหมายและพื้นที่ (Target group & area):** ____ people, ____ villages/sub-districts, linked to which of your 4 hospitals

**5. กิจกรรม (Activities) & timeline:** training → campaigns → screening days → referral follow-up → evaluation

**6. ตัวชี้วัดและการประเมินผล (Indicators & evaluation)**
| Indicator | Baseline | Target | Data source |
|---|---|---|---|
| People screened | 0 | ____ | App records |
| Positive screens referred and seen by specialist | – | ____% | Hospital records |
| Volunteers trained | 0 | ____ | Attendance |
| People counselled on tobacco/alcohol/betel | 0 | ____ | Forms |

**7. ความยั่งยืน (Sustainability):** how local health offices and the sub-district fund (กองทุนหลักประกันสุขภาพตำบล) can continue the work
