## ⚠️ Fit – read first
- Data **must already exist**. You may not collect new data from participants, or generate new data from existing specimens. Up to **10% of the budget** may go to new data generation, **only to validate key findings**.
- The purpose is to study **cancer etiology, incidence, natural history, risk or outcomes**. **Medical image analysis is an explicit priority** ("advance image analysis and extraction of behavioural and environmental information").
- **Good framing:** use your 4 hospitals' **archived** oral photographs, histopathology and clinical records to study **natural history and risk of malignant transformation of oral potentially malignant disorders (OPMD)** using AI image features combined with risk factors (betel, tobacco, alcohol). This is a risk/outcomes question that uses AI, not only "building a classifier".
- Not responsive: new data collection beyond validation, continuing an ongoing analysis, or maintaining/distributing datasets.
