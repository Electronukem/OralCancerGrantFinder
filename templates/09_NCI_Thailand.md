# #8 National Cancer Institute of Thailand / Department of Medical Services – Partnership Template

**Why it fits:** NCI Thailand (สถาบันมะเร็งแห่งชาติ) is better approached as a **partner** than as a grant to apply for. Its endorsement, cancer-registry data, and route to national screening policy strengthen every other application. It may also co-fund through Department of Medical Services (กรมการแพทย์) research budgets **[CONFIRM]**.
**Website:** https://www.nci.go.th
**Approach:** formal letter to the Director plus a meeting. A Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) or letter of support follows.

---

## A. What to ask for (choose)
- ☐ **Letter of support** for NRCT / HSRI / NIH / Wellcome applications
- ☐ **Co-investigator** from NCI Thailand (e.g. head-and-neck oncology, cancer registry)
- ☐ **Data access:** Thai Cancer Registry statistics for your provinces (stage at diagnosis, incidence)
- ☐ **Pathway to policy:** inclusion of AI-assisted oral screening in national cancer control planning
- ☐ **Co-funding** from DMS research budget or in-kind (staff, venues) **[CONFIRM]**

## B. Documents to prepare
| Document | Fill from | Done |

## C. Request letter – fill-in (Thai official letter format)
- **เรื่อง (Subject):** ขอความร่วมมือและหนังสือสนับสนุนโครงการวิจัย ____
- **เรียน (To):** ผู้อำนวยการสถาบันมะเร็งแห่งชาติ
- **Paragraph 1:** who you are; the 4-hospital network; the project in one sentence
- **Paragraph 2:** why it matters nationally (late-stage %, mortality)
- **Paragraph 3:** the specific requests (from list A) and the funders you are applying to, with deadlines
- **Paragraph 4:** what NCI Thailand gains (co-authorship, data for national planning, a pilot for a national programme)
- **Contact:** ____

## D. Draft letter of support (for NCI Thailand to adapt)
> "The National Cancer Institute of Thailand supports the project '____' led by ____ in collaboration with ____ , ____ and ____ hospitals. Oral cancer remains a significant burden in Thailand, and most cases are diagnosed at a late stage. The proposed AI-assisted early detection approach aligns with our national cancer control priorities. We will [provide ____ / participate as ____ / consider the findings for ____]. Signed ____, Director."

## E. Tips
- Arrive with a **specific, small request** and a draft letter.
- Offer **co-authorship** and use of findings for national planning.
- Follow up in writing within one week.
