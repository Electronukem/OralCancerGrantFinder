## ⚠️ Does your project fit? Read first
IMAT funds **new technologies** (assays, devices, instruments, platforms) for cancer analysis, including **early detection and screening**. But it **excludes**:
- projects **focused primarily on software, informatics or machine learning**
- **existing** technologies already proven in a cancer-relevant system
- instrumentation for **whole-body / in vivo imaging**
- projects without **numerical performance targets**

**Fits only if** your innovation is a **new detection device or assay** (e.g. a low-cost intraoral imaging module combining autofluorescence / multispectral / polarised light with a smartphone, a salivary biomarker assay, a brush-biopsy cytology device), with AI as a supporting component.
**Does not fit** if the project is "train a deep-learning model on smartphone photos". Use NRCT/PMU (02, 04) or ITCR (13) for that.

## Review emphasis
Innovation and the conceptual framework weigh most for R61. Performance Measures are an **additional review criterion**: they must be quantitative and sufficient to justify a follow-on **R33** (template 12).
