# #21 Pfizer Global Medical Grants / Industry Oncology Grants – Application Template

**Why lower fit:** industry independent-grant programmes usually issue **topic-specific Requests for Proposals (RFPs)**, often about treatment, quality improvement or medical education in specific cancers. Apply **only when an RFP matches** (e.g. early detection, cancer-care quality in Asia, or digital health).
**Website:** https://www.pfizer.com/about/programs-policies/grants · **Submission:** company grant portal **[CONFIRM]**
Other companies with similar programmes: oncology-focused firms running independent research or quality-improvement grants in Asia-Pacific **[check each company's grants page]**

## A. Eligibility check
- ☐ An **open RFP** matches early detection / oral or head-and-neck cancer / digital health in your region
- ☐ Your institution can accept industry funding (conflict-of-interest and anti-kickback rules; hospital policy)
- ☐ The project is **independent**: the company does not control design or publication

## B. Typical process [CONFIRM per RFP]
1. **Letter of Intent (LOI)** or short proposal via the portal → 2. Invitation to a full proposal → 3. Review → 4. Agreement

## D. Tips
- Mirror the **RFP's exact wording** in your objectives.
- Emphasise **measurable patient-care outcomes** (earlier stage at diagnosis, referral times).
- Industry reviewers like **quality-improvement framing** and clear metrics.
