# #15 IARC (International Agency for Research on Cancer, WHO) – Collaboration & Fellowship Template

**Why it fits:** IARC has a long record of oral cancer screening research in South and Southeast Asia. A collaboration rarely brings large direct funding, but it gives **scientific credibility**, methodology, possible co-funding, and a strong co-applicant for Wellcome, NIH or e-ASIA.
**Website:** https://www.iarc.who.int
**Two routes:**
1. **Research collaboration** with the relevant IARC team (e.g. early detection, prevention & infection; screening; cancer surveillance) **[CONFIRM team names]**
2. **IARC fellowships / training** for early-career researchers, especially from LMICs **[CONFIRM current programmes and deadlines]**

---

## Route 1 – Collaboration proposal (send to the IARC scientist/team leader)

**Before contacting:** identify 1–2 IARC scientists who have published on oral cancer screening or early detection; read their recent papers.

**Email + 2-page concept note – fill-in**
1. **Subject:** "Collaboration proposal – AI-assisted oral cancer early detection, 4-hospital network, Thailand"
2. **Who we are:** PI, 4 hospitals, annual case volume, pathology capacity (CP §2–3)
3. **What we propose:** objectives and design (CP §5–6), half a page
4. **Why IARC:** link to their specific prior work: ____
5. **What we ask:** ☐ methodological advice ☐ co-investigator on grants ☐ protocol harmonisation with IARC screening standards ☐ training ☐ data analysis support
6. **What we offer:** data from a high-burden setting, co-authorship, a field site for validation
7. **Funding plan:** list the grants you will apply to together (NIH, Wellcome, e-ASIA)
8. **Attachments:** PI CV, 1-page project summary

**If they agree, request:** a letter of collaboration for grant applications (CP §12) and possibly a formal collaboration agreement.

## Tips
- Contact **a named scientist**, not a general inbox.
- Offer something concrete that IARC gains (data, field site, a high-burden population).
- Start 6–12 months before the grant deadlines you want IARC on.
