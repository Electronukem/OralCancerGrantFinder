# #20 Japan AMED / Korea KHIDI – Application Template

Both **require a lead institution in that country**. Your role is the **Thai partner**. The main task is finding and convincing a Japanese or Korean PI.

---

## Part A – Japan: AMED (incl. SATREPS) [CONFIRM all]
**Website:** https://www.amed.go.jp/en/
**Key programme: SATREPS** (Science and Technology Research Partnership for Sustainable Development) is run jointly by **AMED/JST** (funding the Japanese side) and **JICA** (funding the partner-country side as ODA technical cooperation).

### How it works
1. A **Japanese PI** applies to AMED/JST.
2. **At the same time**, the **Thai counterpart institution must submit an official request for technical cooperation through the Thai government's ODA channel** (Thailand International Cooperation Agency, TICA) to the Japanese government/JICA, **by the deadline in that year's SATREPS call**. Without this government request, the project cannot be adopted.
3. Both are reviewed; projects typically run ~5 years.

### Checklist
| Item | Who | Done |
|---|---|---|
| Japanese partner PI identified (AI/medical imaging/oral surgery group) | You | ☐ |
| Joint research plan (English; the Japanese side also prepares Japanese forms) | Both | ☐ |
| **Thai official ODA request form via TICA** (deadline usually several months before adoption) | Thai institution | ☐ |
| Thai institution commitment: counterpart staff, facilities, operating budget | Thai institution | ☐ |
| Thai-side CVs | CP §3 | ☐ |
| Ethics & data-sharing plan (Thailand ↔ Japan transfer; Thai PDPA + Japanese APPI) | Both | ☐ |

### Fill-in: joint plan outline
1. Title; 2. Japanese & Thai teams; 3. Background (CP §4); 4. Objectives & outputs; 5. Activities by year; 6. **Capacity development in Thailand** (SATREPS values this heavily); 7. Social implementation plan; 8. Equipment Japan will provide; 9. Thai inputs.

## Part B – Korea: KHIDI [CONFIRM all]
**Website:** https://www.khidi.or.kr/eps
**How it works:** a **Korean institution leads** an international joint R&D project in health technology; the Thai partner contributes clinical sites and data.

### Checklist
| Item | Done |
|---|---|
| Korean lead PI / company identified | ☐ |
| Joint research agreement (roles, IP, data) | ☐ |
| Thai letters of commitment (4 hospitals) | ☐ |
| Thai-side work plan and (if funded) budget | ☐ |
| Ethics approvals + cross-border data plan | ☐ |

## Tips
- Find partners by searching co-authors on oral-cancer AI papers from Japanese/Korean groups, or ask NECTEC/NSTDA, who have Japanese/Korean links.
- For SATREPS, **start 12+ months ahead** because of the government request step.
