## ⚠️ Fit
Same scope limits as R61 (template 11): a **technology/device/assay**, **not primarily software or ML**. R33 is the **next step** after a successful R61 or equivalent pilot: you already have a working prototype and must now **refine it and validate it** in cancer-relevant samples or settings.
