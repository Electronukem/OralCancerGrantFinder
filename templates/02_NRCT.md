# #1 National Research Council of Thailand (NRCT / วช.) – Application Template

**Why it fits:** Thailand's main national research funder. A Thai PI at a partner hospital applies directly, and the research addresses a major national health problem.
**Website:** https://www.nrct.go.th · **Submission system:** NRIIS (https://nriis.go.th)
**Language:** Thai (with English title and abstract)
**Typical cycle:** Calls for next-fiscal-year funding are usually announced around **Nov–Apr**. FY2571 (Oct 2027–Sep 2028) calls are expected roughly Nov 2026–Apr 2027 **[CONFIRM]**. Your institution's internal deadline comes 1–2 weeks earlier.

---

## A. Eligibility check

- ☐ PI is a Thai-national researcher employed at a Thai government agency, university, or state hospital **[CONFIRM per call]**
- ☐ PI and all co-investigators are registered in NRIIS
- ☐ Lead institution is registered in NRIIS and its research office can certify (รับรอง) the proposal
- ☐ The project topic matches one of the call's programmes (แผนงาน / ประเด็น). For example, medical & public health, ageing society, AI / digital technology, or NCD prevention
- ☐ The PI is within the call's limit on concurrent projects **[CONFIRM]**
- ☐ No overlap with funding you receive from another Thai funder for the same work

## F. Scoring tips
- Put **numbers in every outcome**: images, sensitivity targets, people screened, screeners trained.
- Show **national impact**: link to MOPH NCD strategy, Thai cancer control plan, and national AI strategy.
- Show **who will use it** after the project, and include a letter from a provincial health office or NCI Thailand.
- Explain why **4 hospitals in different regions** make the result generalisable.
- Keep the methodology realistic for the time and budget.
