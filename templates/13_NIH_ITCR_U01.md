## ⚠️ Fit – read first
ITCR funds **informatics tools for the cancer research community**, from prototype to first dissemination. AI/ML tools are in scope, and "clinical decision support" is listed. However:
- The tool must be **used by other cancer researchers or clinicians**, with **end-user engagement** and **collaborator letters**.
- **Data generation may not exceed 15% of the budget in any year.** You cannot fund large-scale image collection here. Collect data with Thai funding (02–05), then use ITCR to build the **open tool**.
- Projects that mainly **apply existing methods** are not responsive.

**Best framing:** an **open-source platform or toolkit** for oral-lesion image analysis (standardised acquisition app + annotation tool + trained, documented models + model card) that other oral-cancer research groups worldwide can use and adapt. **Confirm fit with the NCI ITCR contact before writing.**
