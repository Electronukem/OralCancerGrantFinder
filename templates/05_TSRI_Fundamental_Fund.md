# #4 TSRI Fundamental Fund (FF / กองทุนส่งเสริม ววน. – ทุนสนับสนุนงานมูลฐาน) – Application Template

**Why it fits:** National research money that TSRI allocates to **institutions**, which then fund their own researchers. You compete only within your hospital or university, so the odds are better, but grants are smaller and usually run one year at a time.
**Website:** https://www.tsri.or.th
**Submission:** through your **institution's research office**, then NRIIS (Thai)
**Typical cycle:** the institution announces an internal call for next-fiscal-year FF proposals, often **Oct–Jan** **[CONFIRM with your research office now. The FY2571 internal call may open soon.]**

---

## A. Eligibility check
- ☐ Your hospital or affiliated university/medical school **receives an FF allocation**. Ask the research office. University hospitals and military medical colleges often do; some MOPH hospitals do not.
- ☐ The project fits the **institution's strategic research plan** (FF projects must align with it)
- ☐ PI is a staff member of that institution
- ☐ Within the internal limit on number of projects or budget per PI **[CONFIRM]**

## D. Fill-in template
Same NRIIS headings as the NRCT template (title, rationale, objectives, methods, plan, budget, outputs/outcomes/impact, TRL/SRL), plus:

**Alignment with institutional strategy (ความสอดคล้องกับยุทธศาสตร์หน่วยงาน):**
"This project supports [institution]'s strategic goal ____ by ____."

**Year-1 scope:** FF is usually funded year by year, so define a **self-contained first-year deliverable**, e.g. "Annotated dataset of ____ images from 4 hospitals + baseline model with ≥ __% sensitivity". This is ideal seed funding for pilot data that strengthens the NIH and Wellcome applications.

## E. Budget template
Thai government categories (see the NRCT template). Keep Year 1 within the internal ceiling **[CONFIRM]**.

## F. Scoring tips
- Present it as **seed funding that leverages larger grants**: list the NIH, NRCT and HSRI applications it enables.
- Match the institution's strategic wording exactly.
- Promise **concrete outputs in 12 months**: dataset, publication, prototype.
