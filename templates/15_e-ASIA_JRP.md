# #14 e-ASIA Joint Research Program (e-ASIA JRP) – Application Template

**Why it fits:** a multilateral programme in which **each country's team is funded by its own national agency**. Thailand is a member; the Thai team is funded through a Thai member agency (e.g. NRCT / TSRI / NSTDA **[CONFIRM which Thai agency participates in the call]**).
**Website:** https://www.the-easia.org/jrp/
**Cycle:** usually one call per year with topics that change each time (health research topics have included cancer and NCDs) **[CONFIRM current call topic includes cancer / health / AI]**

---

## A. Eligibility check
- ☐ Consortium of researchers from **at least 3 member countries** (e.g. Thailand + Japan + Vietnam/Malaysia/Philippines/Indonesia/Cambodia/Myanmar/Laos), **each with a funding agency participating in that specific call** **[CONFIRM list]**
- ☐ The call topic matches (e.g. health research → cancer / NCD / digital health)
- ☐ Each country's PI meets their own agency's eligibility rules
- ☐ A lead PI is chosen to coordinate the joint proposal

## B. What gets submitted
| Document | Who submits | Language | Done |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Joint proposal** (one common document, same for all agencies) | Each PI submits the same document to their own agency (sometimes plus central submission) | English | ☐ |
| **National application form** for each agency (Thai side: via NRIIS / agency form) | Each country PI | National language (Thai side: Thai + English) | ☐ |
| Budget per country (each agency funds its own team) | Each PI | | ☐ |
| CVs for each country team | All | English | ☐ |
| Consortium agreement / letters of commitment | All | English | ☐ |

**All agencies must approve the project for it to be funded.** If one agency declines, the whole project usually fails.

## D. Tips
- Choose partners who **already work together** or have co-authored papers.
- Make the **multi-country design essential**, not decorative.
- Line up all national submissions on the **same timetable**. Agency deadlines may differ.
