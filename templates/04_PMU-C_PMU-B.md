# #3 Program Management Units: PMU-C (บพข.) / PMU-B (บพค.) – Application Template

**Why it fits:**
- **PMU-C** (Program Management Unit for Competitiveness, บพข.) funds research that becomes **products and industry**, including medical devices, digital health and AI. Use it if your goal is a deployable AI screening app or device.
- **PMU-B** (Program Management Unit for Human Resources & Institutional Development, บพค.) funds **research teams, researchers and capacity**, e.g. frontier research, talent, and postdocs. Use it to build the team and research capability.

**Websites:** https://www.pmuc.or.th · https://www.pmub.or.th
**Submission:** NRIIS (Thai). Some programmes have a concept-note stage first.
**Typical cycle:** annual calls for next-fiscal-year funding, often around **Nov–Mar** **[CONFIRM]**.

---

## A. Eligibility check
**PMU-C**
- ☐ Project targets a **marketable product/service** with a realistic route to users
- ☐ A **private-sector partner** is usually expected, often with **co-funding (in-cash / in-kind)** **[CONFIRM required %]**
- ☐ Clear TRL progression (e.g. TRL 4 → 7)
- ☐ Fits a programme platform, e.g. medical & health industry, or digital / AI **[CONFIRM programme names]**

**PMU-B**
- ☐ Fits a specific programme: research-team development, frontier research, young researchers, postdoc, etc. **[CONFIRM]**
- ☐ PI meets that programme's career-stage requirements
- ☐ Shows how the funding builds lasting **human capacity** (students, postdocs, new skills)

## D. Fill-in template

**Common sections:** title, rationale, objectives, methods, timeline, budget, outputs/outcomes/impact, TRL/SRL (all from the CP; see the NRCT template for Thai headings)

**PMU-C extra sections**
- **Product description:** what the user receives (app? cloud service? device?) ____
- **Target users & customers:** who pays (hospitals, NHSO, private dental chains)? ____
- **Market size:** number of dental clinics/hospitals in Thailand and ASEAN, screening volume ____
- **Competitors & advantage:** existing oral AI tools; your edge is Thai data, accuracy, cost, offline use ____
- **Regulatory path:** Thai FDA (อย.) Software as a Medical Device class, required clinical evidence, timeline ____
- **IP & licensing:** who owns the model, data and app; licensing to the partner ____
- **Commercialisation milestones:** prototype → clinical validation → FDA registration → first paying site ____
- **Private partner contribution:** in-cash THB ____ + in-kind (engineering, devices) THB ____

**PMU-B extra sections**
- **People developed:** ____ PhD/MSc students, ____ postdocs, ____ hospital staff trained in AI research
- **Team-building plan:** mentoring, international collaboration, joint publications
- **Long-term research programme:** where the team will be in 5 years

## F. Scoring tips
- PMU-C reviewers want **evidence of demand**: letters from hospitals or clinics willing to pilot or buy.
- Show a **realistic regulatory plan**. Many AI health projects stop at "we built a model".
- PMU-B reviewers want **people outcomes**: students graduated, skills gained.
