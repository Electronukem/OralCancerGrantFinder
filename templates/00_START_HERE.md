# Grant Application Templates – AI Early Oral Cancer Detection (Thailand)

Prepared 12 Oct 2026. One template per funder, numbered in the same order as the "most likely to work" ranking.

## How to use these templates

1. **Fill in `01_Common_Information_Pack` first.** About 70% of what every funder asks for is the same: team, hospitals, problem, aims, methods, budget, ethics. Each grant template points back to the section of the Common Pack to copy from ("→ CP §4").
2. **Then open the template for the grant you are applying to.** Each one contains:
   - **Eligibility check** – confirm you can apply before doing any work
   - **Registrations & approvals** – things that take weeks, so start them first
   - **Document checklist** – every file to submit, with page limits
   - **Section-by-section fill-in template** – what each section must say
   - **Budget template** – the categories that funder uses
   - **Scoring tips**
   - **Verify before submitting** – items that change from call to call
3. **Always download the official call document (the NOFO, ประกาศ, or call for proposals) for the cycle you apply to.** Page limits, deadlines and forms change every year. Anything marked **[CONFIRM]** is something you must check in the official call.

## Start these NOW (they take the longest)

| Task | Needed for | Typical time |
|---|---|---|
| Register every investigator in **NRIIS** (nriis.go.th) | All Thai funders (#1–4, #14 Thai side) | 1–2 weeks |
| Ethics committee (IRB/EC) approval at **each of the 4 hospitals**, or one central approval accepted by all | All funders that involve patients | 1–3 months |
| **Data-sharing agreement** between the 4 hospitals (Thai PDPA compliant) | All funders | 1–3 months |
| Institution registers for a **UEI in SAM.gov** (foreign entities also need an **NCAGE code**) | All NIH grants (#9–13, #17–18) | 4–8 weeks |
| **Grants.gov** and **eRA Commons** institutional accounts plus a PI account | All NIH grants | 2–4 weeks after SAM |
| **ORCID iD** for every key person, linked to eRA Commons and **SciENcv** | All NIH grants (Common Forms biosketch) | 1 day |
| **Federalwide Assurance (FWA)** for each hospital's ethics committee (OHRP) | NIH, before award | 2–6 weeks |
| Start collecting a **pilot image dataset** and baseline AI accuracy results | Makes every application much stronger | ongoing |

## Important rule changes (2025–2026) that affect you

- **NIH foreign partners:** NIH no longer funds foreign partners through ordinary subawards. For due dates on or after **25 Jan 2026**, applications that request money for foreign components must use NIH's new linked-award structure (**PF5/UF5** activity codes, each foreign institution as its own "International Project Component"). If a US university leads and your Thai hospitals are partners, this applies to you. If a Thai hospital is the applicant itself, **ask the NIH program officer** how the other three Thai hospitals should be included. (NOT-OD-25-104, NOT-OD-25-155)
- **NIH Common Forms:** Since **25 Jan 2026**, biosketches and Current & Pending (Other) Support must be made in **SciENcv**, and every key person must link their ORCID and eRA Commons accounts. (NOT-OD-26-018)
- **NIH and AI-written applications:** NIH does not treat applications "substantially developed by AI" as original, and limits each PI to **6 applications per calendar year**. Write the science yourselves. (NOT-OD-25-132)
- **NVIDIA Academic Grant Program:** not accepting applications as of Oct 2026. Watch for the next call.
- **Thai funders:** almost all submissions go through **NRIIS, in Thai**, and are certified by your institution's research office before the funder's deadline. The institution's internal deadline is usually **1–2 weeks earlier** than the funder's.
