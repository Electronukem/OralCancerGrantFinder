## Fit
✅ **Diagnostic / imaging early-phase trials are in scope**, including optical imaging, radiomics, and clinical lab tests. A **Phase II diagnostic trial** of an AI-assisted optical oral-imaging method fits.
❌ Not in scope: Phase III; observational studies that are not NIH-defined clinical trials; **behavioural trials without a therapeutic endpoint** (→ PAR-25-167, template 18); NCTN/ETCTN/SPORE trials.

**Framing:** "A Phase II multi-site trial of AI-assisted [optical/smartphone] imaging to guide biopsy-site selection and improve early detection of oral cancer in high-risk patients." Prospective; the intervention changes clinical decisions (e.g. biopsy site), so it meets the clinical-trial definition.
