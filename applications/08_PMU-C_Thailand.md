## Product
A software-as-a-service medical device: volunteer mobile app (risk survey + guided photo capture + image-quality check), AI risk scoring (fine-tuned MedGemma + YOLO), and clinician triage dashboard, plus training materials.

## Problem & customer need
See HSRI §1. **Customers:** community and provincial hospitals, Health Region offices, the MOPH, NHSO, and private dental networks; later, export to ASEAN and South Asian health systems.
