## Kon Pop Detection: AI-assisted oral cancer screening for underserved communities

**Organisation:** Kon Pop Detection [FILL: legal name, country], with Mahidol University and [FILL: hospital names], Health Region 10, Thailand

### The problem
Oral cancer kills about 177,000 people a year, mostly in South and Southeast Asia, where betel quid, tobacco and alcohol are common. It is usually curable when found early, but most patients are diagnosed late, when treatment costs about five times more and survival is poor. In Thailand, community screening relies on village health volunteers with about one day of training and a paper form; dentists are scarce and referrals are slow and unsorted.

### Our solution
A smartphone app and dentist dashboard. Volunteers record risk factors and take three mouth photos; AI (Google MedGemma fine-tuned + YOLO lesion detection) checks image quality, marks suspicious areas and scores risk; dentists see high-risk patients first. Low-cost, works with patchy connectivity, and backed by picture-based training and a training game.

### Plan (next 18 months)
1. Validate accuracy against dentist examination and biopsy at multiple hospitals
2. Complete the Health Region 10 pilot; measure referral accuracy, time to diagnosis, usability and costs
3. Expand to Health Regions 7 and 9; engage the MOPH and NHSO on adoption
4. Thai FDA pathway for Software as a Medical Device

### Impact
More early-stage diagnoses; fewer unnecessary trips for patients; better use of scarce dentists; a model for AI-assisted primary-care screening that can transfer to other high-burden countries.
