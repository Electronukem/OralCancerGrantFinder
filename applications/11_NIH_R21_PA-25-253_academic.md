## Title
Feasibility and effectiveness of AI-assisted oral cancer screening delivered by community health volunteers in northeast Thailand

## Specific Aims (draft)
Oral cancer is a leading cause of cancer death in South and Southeast Asia and among some underserved US populations who use areca nut and tobacco. Screening by trained non-specialists can reduce oral cancer mortality, but its effectiveness is limited by variable examiner accuracy, scarce specialists, and weak referral pathways. Thailand's national programme relies on more than a million village health volunteers who screen adults aged 40+ using a paper risk form after about one day of training. A low-cost smartphone tool with AI triage and remote dentist review (Kon Pop Detection) could improve referral accuracy and speed, but its real-world feasibility, effect on the care pathway, and costs are unknown.

**Objective:** to evaluate the feasibility and early effectiveness of AI-assisted volunteer screening with remote triage, compared with standard paper-based screening, in Health Region 10, Thailand.

**Aim 1. Feasibility and acceptability.** Among ~300 volunteers and [FILL] adults screened across [FILL] districts, measure recruitment, image-quality pass rate, time per screen, usability (SUS), and acceptability to volunteers, dentists and patients (mixed methods).

**Aim 2. Care-pathway effectiveness.** Compare AI-assisted districts with matched standard-screening districts [FILL design: stepped-wedge or matched comparison] on (a) positive predictive value of referrals, (b) referral completion, and (c) time from screening to specialist evaluation and biopsy.

**Aim 3. Costs.** Estimate cost per screen and cost per high-risk lesion appropriately referred, from health-system and patient perspectives, to inform a future R01 pragmatic trial and policy decisions.

**Impact:** effect sizes, feasibility parameters and costs to design a definitive pragmatic trial (PAR-25-167 successor), and a model for AI-assisted cancer screening by community health workers transferable to other low-resource and underserved settings.
