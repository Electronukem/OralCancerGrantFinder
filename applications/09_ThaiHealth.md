# ThaiHealth (สสส.) – Draft Proposal: Community Oral Cancer Risk Reduction and Volunteer Capacity Building in Health Region 10

**Why ThaiHealth:** ThaiHealth funds **health promotion and prevention**, not AI development. This proposal funds the **volunteer training, community awareness and risk-factor reduction** (betel quid, tobacco, alcohol) around the pilot. The app is a tool within that work.
**Applicant:** a Thai organisation: hospital, Mahidol University, community organisation or foundation **[CONFIRM eligible applicant types and the open-grant channel at thaihealth.or.th]**
**Language:** Thai

**EN:** Building volunteer and community capacity to reduce risk factors and screen for oral cancer with digital tools, Health Region 10

## หลักการและเหตุผล (Rationale)
- Betel quid, tobacco and alcohol use in the northeast [FILL: local data]
- Oral cancer is often found late; volunteers have about one day of training and paper forms
- Opportunity: train volunteers to (1) give brief advice on quitting betel, tobacco and alcohol; (2) screen with a simple app; (3) follow up referrals

## วัตถุประสงค์ (Objectives)
1. Train 20 lead volunteer trainers and ~300 volunteers in oral cancer risk communication, brief cessation advice and app-based screening
2. Screen [FILL: N] adults aged 40+ in [FILL: N] sub-districts, and refer and follow up all high-risk people
3. Deliver community campaigns on betel quid, tobacco and alcohol in [FILL] villages
4. Link people who want to quit to cessation services (e.g. the Thai national quitline 1600 [CONFIRM] and local clinics)
